Lebanon

Climate change severely impacts Lebanon by causing rising temperatures and shrinking mountain snowpacks, which drastically reduce the country's freshwater supply. These environmental shifts trigger extreme droughts and soil erosion, heavily damaging the agricultural sector and threatening food security. Consequently, these combined water and crop shortages are projected to shrink Lebanon's economy by up to 14% by 2040.

Expand solar energy: Replacing expensive, polluting diesel generators with solar power to cut emissions and lower electricity costs. Modernize farming: Using drip irrigation and planting drought-resistant crops to save water and protect the food supply. Protect natural water: Repairing leaking water networks, treating wastewater, and restoring mountain forests to naturally trap rain and snow.

Grassroots Solar Boom: Out of sheer necessity, citizens and businesses have driven a massive, decentralized solar energy movement, expanding Lebanon's solar capacity past 1,500 Megawatts to escape reliance on polluting, state-run electricity and private diesel generators.
